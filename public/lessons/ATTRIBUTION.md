# Lesson media attribution

All video clips are royalty-free stock (**Pexels** / **Mixkit**). **No YouTube** sources.

Piano note audio layered onto silent stock from **FluidR3 GM** acoustic grand samples
([midi-js-soundfonts / gleitz](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts), FluidR3 license).
Timed to the pitch(es) taught in that step. TR lower-thirds burned into each file.

## Camera policy (M1–M2 demos)

Prefer **top-down / overhead** framing of hands on keys so finger landing points are obvious.
Side/angled concert B-roll is avoided for key-teaching beats. Exception: `m1-l1-sit` keeps a
side posture shot (oturuş / mesafe — not a finger-landing lesson).

## `posture-hands.mp4` / `posture-hands.webm`

Educational loop — correct piano sitting (oturuş). Pexels Charles Parker + Mixkit hand cutaways.

## `demos/` — M1 + M2 step hand demos

1280×720 loops with Turkish captions + AAC/Opus piano audio (when the step teaches pitches).

### Notes

- Stock piano video is typically **silent**; acoustic notes are layered so the taught pitch is audible while TR captions keep the landing target explicit.
- **Pacing:** ~2 s intro hold (hands on keys) → action → ~2 s outro hold. Typical total ~5–7 s. One teaching take (no looping montage).
- **A/V sync:** note-ons are locked to motion peaks on the final cut (`peak − ~1 frame`). If stock shows fewer clear presses than the full phrase, audio is retargeted to the visible presses only.
- Exact single-finger stop on a labeled “Do” key remains approximate when the source clip doesn’t isolate that key — captions + overhead framing + matching pitch teach the intent for MVP.
